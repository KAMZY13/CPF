In Remembrance

Honouring the lives and legacies of two pioneer members of the Comfort Precinct Foundation whose impact continues to shape who we are today.

With deep respect, we honour the lives and legacies of two pioneer members of the Comfort Precinct Foundation whose impact continues to shape who we are today.

Pa Michael Abiodun Sopitan

Died — January 27, 2024

Comrade Benjamin Adebowale Oluwaseyi Akinwunmi

Died- July 28, 2025

Pa Michael Abiodun Sopitan

Former Deputy Director of Finance, Central Bank of Nigeria · Passed January 27, 2024

He was a man of quiet strength, discipline, and clarity. In the early years of the Foundation, his financial guidance and steady presence helped build the structure we stand on today.

Beyond his professional excellence, he believed deeply in service — in showing up, giving, and lifting others where it mattered.

Comrade Benjamin Adebowale Oluwaseyi Akinwunmi

Broadcaster, Ogun State Broadcasting Corporation & Ogun State Television · Passed July 28, 2025

He was a voice that carried truth, compassion, and conviction. Within the Foundation, he used that voice to create awareness, bring people together, and strengthen the mission of reaching those in need.

His presence was not just heard, it was felt.

They were more than members. They were part of the foundation in every sense — shaping its direction, strengthening its purpose, and giving of themselves without hesitation.

Their lives remind us that impact is not measured by position, but by service — by what is given, and who is lifted along the way.

May their souls rest in peace, and may their legacy continue through the work we carry forward.

— Comfort Precinct Foundation
