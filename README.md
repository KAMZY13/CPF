# Comfort Precinct Foundation (CPF)

### A digital home for compassion, community, and change.

Comfort Precinct Foundation (CPF) is a non-governmental charity organization committed to making a meaningful difference through welfare, empowerment, education, and community development.

This project is a fully functional, multi-page website developed to give the foundation an online presence, communicate its mission, and connect its work with the people it serves.

And this isn't just another project sitting in a portfolio. **The website is live, and the foundation actively uses it.**

🌐 **[Visit the Live Website](https://cpf-psi.vercel.app/)**

---

## About the Project

Comfort Precinct Foundation needed a digital space that could represent its mission, share its initiatives, and make information about the organization accessible to the public.

I designed and developed this website to serve that purpose.

The website brings together the foundation's identity, activities, and community-focused initiatives in a simple, accessible, and visually engaging experience.

Built primarily with HTML and CSS, with a little JavaScript for interactivity and animations, this project is an example of taking the fundamentals of web development and putting them to work on a real-world project.

## What CPF Stands For

Comfort Precinct Foundation focuses on making a positive impact in the lives of individuals and communities through:

- **Welfare:** Supporting widows, individuals, and communities through charitable initiatives and social support.
- **Empowerment:** Promoting awareness, personal development, and opportunities that help people improve their lives.
- **Education:** Supporting educational initiatives and creating opportunities for learning and growth.
- **Community Development:** Engaging communities through outreach, public awareness, and initiatives that encourage positive social change.
- **Helping Hands:** Providing assistance and support to members of the foundation.

These areas of focus form the heart of the organization and the purpose behind its online presence.

## What's Inside?

The website is organized into four main pages:

| Page | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Introduces the foundation, its mission, and its initiatives. |
| About | Shares information about the organization and what it stands for. |
| Project | Showcases the foundation's projects and activities. |
| Contact | Provides visitors with a way to reach the foundation. |

There are also two supporting pages linked from the homepage:

- **Memorial:** A dedicated space honoring deceased members of the foundation.
- **Event:** A page featuring news and updates concerning the organization.

These supporting pages complement the main website and provide additional information about the foundation and its community.

## Features

- Fully functional, multi-page website.
- Responsive design for different screen sizes.
- Dedicated pages for the foundation's mission, projects, and contact information.
- Interactive elements and subtle animations.
- Image-based visual content.
- Memorial and organizational event pages linked from the homepage.
- Live deployment through Vercel.
