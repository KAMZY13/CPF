Overview

Who we are and
what we stand for

Comfort Precinct Foundation is a non-governmental organisation founded on October 18, 2018, with a clear and deliberate mission: to stand in the gap for individuals and communities who need support the most.


We operate through structured, constitution-backed initiatives that prioritise dignity, accountability, and measurable outcomes. From prison outreach to widow support and student sponsorship, our work is rooted in the belief that compassion and action belong together.


We are not just an organisation; we are a community of people committed to showing up, doing the work, and leaving lives better than we found them.

Our History

How we got here

Every organisation has a beginning. Ours began with a conviction that the most vulnerable among us deserve more than sympathy, they deserve sustained, structured support.

October 2018

Foundation Established

Comfort Precinct Foundation was officially founded on October 18, 2018. A small team united by shared values, guided by a constitution and a determination to create real, tangible impact in their community.

2019

First Major Projects Launched

The organisation launched its first structured initiatives, reaching out to widows, supporting students, and engaging elderly homes. These early programmes shaped the foundation's identity and approach.

2020 – 2022

Growth Through Challenge

Despite the difficulties of the global pandemic, the foundation continued its work — adapting outreach methods and deepening community relationships. Resilience became a defining characteristic of the team.

2023-2024

Growth That Reached More Lives

The foundation experienced notable financial growth, which translated directly into action. More people received support, and more members of the community were reached with care, assistance, and practical help. A year where growth was felt not just internally, but in people’s lives.

2025 – Present

Scaling Impact

With a growing network of volunteers, trustees, and community partners, the foundation continues to expand its reach — running focused annual projects that create lasting change for those who need it most.

Our Constitution

In this document
  • Name & Identity
  • Mission & Purpose
  • Core Values
  • Membership
  • Governance
  • Finance

Article 1 — Name & Identity

The organisation shall be known as Comfort Precinct Foundation, a non-governmental, non-partisan, and non-profit organisation registered and operating in accordance with applicable Nigerian law. It shall operate under this name and any legally registered abbreviations.

Article 2 — Mission & Purpose

The foundation exists to inform, uplift, and support vulnerable individuals and communities through structured, impactful initiatives. Its purpose is to create real opportunities and lasting change by operating with integrity, accountability, enlightenment and measurable outcomes at the core of every action.

Article 3 — Core Values

The foundation is guided by the following values: Transparency in all operations and reporting; Accountability to donors, beneficiaries, and the wider community; Dignity in every interaction; Impact as the primary measure of success; and Compassion as the foundation of every decision.

Article 4 — Membership

Membership of the foundation is open to individuals who share the organisation's values and mission. Members shall participate in annual activities, contribute to the foundation's goals, and uphold the standards set out in this constitution. Details of membership categories and responsibilities are defined in the full document.

Article 5 — Governance

The foundation shall be governed by a Board of Trustees, responsible for strategic oversight, policy direction, and ensuring the organisation fulfils its mission. The board shall meet at minimum twice per year and shall operate in accordance with democratic principles and the best interests of the communities the foundation serves.

Article 6 — Finance & Resources

All financial resources of the foundation shall be used solely for the purposes outlined in this constitution. Financial records shall be maintained transparently, reviewed annually, and made available to members and stakeholders upon request. No member of the board shall personally benefit from the foundation's funds.

Our Co-Working Space

Our co-working space is a modest, shared environment where members come together to work, connect, and support one another. It is not a formal office, it is simply a space that belongs to the community.

The heart of it is the people. Members look out for each other, share ideas, and collaborate on projects. Whether you need a quiet place to work or just the company of others who share your values, there is a seat here for you.

What We Do

Our Programme Areas

Our work is organised into five programme areas, each addressing a specific and real need. Together they reflect our commitment to serving communities from the inside out.

Welfare

Providing direct care and essential support to individuals and groups who need it most, with dignity at the centre of every interaction.

  • Prison outreach
  • Support for the less privileged
  • Visits to motherless homes
Welfare

Empowerment

Building awareness and creating pathways for individuals and communities to become more self-reliant and informed.

  • Awareness in schools, churches & public spaces
  • Widow empowerment
  • Orphan support
Empowerment

Education

Keeping young people in school and giving them the tools and support they need to build a better future for themselves.

  • Student sponsorship
  • Support for primary and secondary schools
Education

Development

Investing in the long-term growth of the communities we serve through practical initiatives and collaborative efforts.

  • Community development initiatives
  • General outreach and improvement efforts
Development

Helping Hands

We believe that a foundation is only as strong as the people within it. Helping Hands exists to ensure that our own members are cared for, practically and personally.

  • Financial assistance for members in need
  • Support for members' small businesses
  • Standing with members through difficult times
