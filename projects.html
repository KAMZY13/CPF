Our Work Right Now

These are the lives we are walking alongside in 2026 — four focused initiatives, each addressing a distinct but urgent human need.

Active · 2026

Prison Outreach Programme

Providing essential items such as toiletries and blankets to inmates, alongside awareness and support sessions to promote dignity and rehabilitation. Because incarceration should never mean abandonment.

Active · 2026

Operation Feed the Widows

Continuing our commitment to supporting widows through food assistance and empowerment initiatives that promote stability and independence. No widow should be left unseen or unsupported.

Active · 2026

Student Sponsorship Programme

Supporting underprivileged students in public primary and secondary schools with resources and financial assistance to keep them in school. No child should be denied a future because of where they started.

Active · 2026

Women Empowerment Initiative

Supporting women with small businesses through direct financial assistance and practical guidance. When a woman is equipped to sustain herself, her family and her community feel the difference.

Previous Projects

Every past project is proof that showing up consistently creates real change. This is a record of our impact over the years — the communities touched, the lives reached, and the work that built the foundation we stand on today.

2018

In October 2018, during the foundation's anniversary, Comfort Precinct Organisation carried out one of its earliest outreach efforts with a visit to a destitute home. The team spent time engaging with residents, providing essential support items, and sharing moments of care and attention. This outreach, though modest, laid the foundation for what would become a consistent commitment to reaching the most vulnerable members of society and marked the beginning of the organization's hands-on humanitarian work.

Welfare Community
2019

In 2019, the foundation expanded its reach across multiple communities. Early in the year, visits were made to elderly homes where gifts and support items were distributed, offering both material aid and companionship to senior citizens. In June, the organization empowered ten widows in Ota, Ogun State, providing support aimed at improving their living conditions and independence. Later in October, during its anniversary, the foundation visited Mount Olivet Grammar School, New Bodija, Ibadan, where four indigent students were sponsored, reinforcing its commitment to education and youth development.

Welfare Empowerment Education
2020

Despite the challenges of the year, the foundation remained active in 2020. In February, members visited the Ijamido Orphanage in Ota, extending care and support to children through donations and engagement. In June, the organization carried out "Operation Feed the Widows" in Ile-Ife, providing food and relief materials to widows in the community. By October, during the anniversary period, ten women were empowered through targeted support initiatives, continuing the organization's focus on uplifting vulnerable groups.

Welfare Empowerment Operation Feed the Widows
2021

The year 2021 marked a significant expansion in impact. In February, "Operation Feed the Widows" was held in Itire, Lagos, where 100 widows received food supplies valued at ₦30,000 each, providing substantial relief. In June, the foundation sponsored indigent students at Ayegun Community High School in Ibadan, supporting their educational journey. By October, the organization turned inward as well, empowering widows and less privileged members within the foundation itself, reinforcing its belief in supporting both the community and its own members.

Operation Feed the Widows Education Empowerment
2022

In 2022, the foundation continued its focus on education and empowerment. In February, five students at Adekile Goodwill Grammar School in Ibadan had their WAEC fees fully sponsored, ensuring they could complete their secondary education without financial barriers. In June, the organization carried out a women empowerment initiative aimed at providing support and encouragement to women in need. Later in October, "Operation Feed the Widows" was once again organized, continuing its consistent effort to provide relief to widows across communities.

Education Empowerment Operation Feed the Widows
2023

The foundation sustained its outreach efforts in 2023 across different areas. In February, a visit was made to an orphanage, where children received support, care, and essential items. In June, the organization sponsored students at Premier Grammar School in Lafenwa, Abeokuta, helping to ease the financial burden on families. By October, another empowerment program was conducted, continuing the foundation's mission of uplifting individuals and strengthening communities through direct intervention.

Welfare Education Empowerment
2024

In 2024, the scale of support continued to grow. In February, five women were empowered with financial support of ₦100,000 each, helping them strengthen their businesses and livelihoods. In June, "Operation Feed the Widows" was carried out in Ibadan, where 100 widows received food supplies valued at ₦35,000 each. Later in October, the same initiative was extended to Ekiti State, again supporting 100 widows with similar relief packages, demonstrating consistency and expansion in outreach efforts.

Empowerment Operation Feed the Widows
2025

The year 2025 reflected both educational support and community impact. In February, 1,000 exercise books were distributed to students at Itolu Grammar School, alongside financial empowerment of a foundation member with ₦100,000. In June, the organization sponsored two students' WAEC fees at the same school, ensuring continuity in their education. The foundation also visited a special needs children's home in Ilaro, providing gifts and financial assistance. In October, a memorial lecture was held in honor of Comrade Debo Akinwunmi at his alma mater, where exercise books were distributed to students, blending remembrance with meaningful community support.
